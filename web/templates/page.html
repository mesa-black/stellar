A website proves who it is with a public key. Its fingerprint is 43 characters nobody reads. Stellar draws those characters as a constellation with a name, so a change is something you can see.

  1. Stellar asks the site for its certificate and hashes the public key inside.
  2. The hash decides every star: where it sits, how bright it is, what it joins.
  3. Your browser remembers the sky. Come back later and it tells you whether it is still the same.

A site name shows the sky of the key that site answers with. A document signed with other keys has another sky: to see that one, paste the SHA256: line the document prints.
